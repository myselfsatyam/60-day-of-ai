# Working of Chatbot:
Working of chatbot is break down in few steps of understand its working properly.
Step 1: User send a input message to chatbot.
Step 2: The text sent to chatbot is converted into small units(tokens) so, AI model can process it , this process is called tokenisation.
Step 3: The language model analyzes the context and predicts the desired output on training data.
Step 4: The system generates a complete response sentence by sentence.
Step 5: Reponse is sent back to user through the interface
